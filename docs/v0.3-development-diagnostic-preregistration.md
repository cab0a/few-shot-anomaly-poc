## English Summary

This document preregisters a development-only diagnostic study after the completed v0.2 `REJECT` decision. It permits a blinded visual observation of only the 28 unique assets mechanically selected in v0.2.7 and a controlled normal-score stability probe on 60 deterministically selected development images. It does not authorize tuning, rescoring, or re-evaluating the completed v0.2 final test.

## Status and Controlling Identity

**Status: preregistered; no diagnostic image has been opened and no diagnostic score has been computed.**

The preregistration ID is `v0.3.0-pcb2-development-diagnostic-1`. The first pushed Git commit containing this document is the controlling preregistration commit. The next stage must record that full commit and this document's SHA-256 before it may resolve a selected asset to image bytes or create a diagnostic score.

This document creates a new development study. It does not amend `v0.2-pcb2-method-evaluation-1`, reopen run `visa-pcb2-v0-2-final`, or change any v0.2 artifact, threshold, metric, gate, method, or decision.

## Diagnostic Question

After all three fixed v0.2 methods were rejected, is there one bounded and observable input-condition hypothesis that is supported by both:

1. repeated visible observations among the already selected v0.2.7 errors; and
2. a controlled normal-score response under a matching development-only perturbation?

The diagnostic can prioritize one hypothesis for a separate experiment. It cannot demonstrate anomaly-detection performance, establish causality, or reverse the v0.2 decision.

## Development-Only Intended Use

The study may:

- describe visible properties of the mechanically selected errors without claiming their cause;
- measure paired score changes under fixed controlled perturbations of development-only normal images;
- identify whether framing, global exposure, or blur deserves one separately preregistered follow-up; and
- record a negative or inconclusive diagnostic as a valid result.

The hypothetical context remains a CPU-only, normal-only visual-inspection PoC. This is not a customer investigation, production incident analysis, or acceptance test.

### First-Pass Blinding

Images are reviewed once in opaque asset-ID ascending order. During the first-pass observation, the reviewer may see only:

- the opaque asset ID;
- the image pixels at their original decoded extent; and
- the fixed observation form.

The reviewer must not see the method, case type, true class, prediction, anomaly score, threshold, margin, rank, source path, or another method's selection status. The review runner must not expose directory names or filenames containing class semantics. Method and error metadata are joined only after all 28 first-pass observation records are immutable.

This is single-reviewer blinding, not an inter-rater study. The image content may still make an anomaly visually apparent, so the protocol does not claim complete diagnostic blinding.

### Fixed Observation Fields

Each unique asset receives exactly one record with:

- `asset_id`;
- `review_status`: `reviewed` or `unreadable`;
- `framing_or_crop_difference_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `pose_or_registration_offset_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `global_exposure_difference_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `blur_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `localized_change_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `diffuse_change_visible`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `no_obvious_visible_change`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`;
- `visible_observation`: a factual note of at most 300 characters; and
- `inferred_cause`: an optional, explicitly labeled hypothesis of at most 300 characters.

The form does not ask whether the method was correct, whether the image should pass inspection, or whether a visual feature caused a score. A visible observation and an inferred cause must remain separate fields.

### Allowed Review Outputs

The public study may commit the observation records and their later deterministic join to the 29 fixed method-case records. VisA image pixels, crops, thumbnails, overlays, and montages are not published by default.

## Diagnostic Track B: Controlled Normal-Score Stability

### Fixed Development Pool and Selection

The input pool is the 881 normal calibration records already used as development data in v0.2. These images are not untouched validation data and must never be described as such. The 20 v0.2 reference records and every v0.2 final-test record are excluded.

Exactly 60 base images are selected by sorting the 881 POSIX relative paths by the lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 digest of this UTF-8 byte string:

```text
SHA-256("few-shot-anomaly-poc:v0.3.0:pcb2:normal-diagnostic:42:<posix_relative_path>")
```

Ties are resolved by POSIX relative path ascending. The first 60 records form the complete development diagnostic partition. Selection occurs before image decoding and must be serialized and hash-bound before scoring.

### Fixed Conditions

Each base image produces exactly five diagnostic conditions from the same decoded BGR `uint8` array:

| Condition | Fixed operation |
| --- | --- |
| `control` | unchanged decoded array |
| `brightness_085` | multiply every channel by `0.85`, round with `numpy.rint`, clip to `[0, 255]`, cast to `uint8` |
| `brightness_115` | multiply every channel by `1.15`, round with `numpy.rint`, clip to `[0, 255]`, cast to `uint8` |
| `gaussian_blur` | OpenCV Gaussian blur, kernel `5 x 5`, sigma `1.0`, `BORDER_REFLECT_101` |
| `translate_4_4` | translate four pixels right and four pixels down with linear interpolation and `BORDER_REFLECT_101` |

There is no random augmentation, crop, rotation, perspective warp, mask, anomaly synthesis, or adaptive severity. Transform code, OpenCV/NumPy identities, serialization rules, and input hashes must be fixed in the next machine-readable contract before execution.

### Fixed Methods and State

The probe reuses the three completed v0.2 methods, their exact fitted-state hashes, configurations, preprocessing, score definitions, and normal-only thresholds. It may load the existing ignored fitted state after verifying its recorded SHA-256. It must not refit, update a memory bank, recalibrate a threshold, train on observations, or inspect v0.2 final-test scores while implementing the probe.

The probe produces 60 base images × 5 conditions × 3 methods = 900 score records. It is a development sensitivity check, not another CPU benchmark. Latency is not measured.

### Fixed Summaries

For each method and non-control condition, calculate:

- attempted, successful, and failed score counts;
- median paired signed score delta from control;
- median paired absolute score delta;
- nearest-rank p95 paired absolute score delta;
- count and rate above the unchanged v0.2 threshold;
- normal-to-anomalous threshold-crossing count among control-normal pairs; and
- anomalous-to-normal threshold-crossing count among control-anomalous pairs.

Report the control count and above-threshold rate separately. Do not calculate AUROC, AUPRC, recall, precision, accuracy, an anomaly confusion matrix, or a customer acceptance metric from this normal-only diagnostic set.

## Diagnostic Signal Rule

Three signal families are eligible:

| Priority | Visual tag | Matching controlled conditions |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | framing or pose/registration difference | `translate_4_4` |
| 2 | global exposure difference | `brightness_085`, `brightness_115` |
| 3 | visible blur | `gaussian_blur` |

A family is `supported` only when both conditions hold for at least one method:

1. the matching visual tag is `yes` in at least three unique selected assets associated with that method; and
2. at least one matching controlled condition either produces at least six normal-to-anomalous crossings among the 60 base pairs or has a median paired absolute score delta at least `0.10 ×` that method's unchanged v0.2 threshold.

For priority 1, an asset counts once when either `framing_or_crop_difference_visible` or `pose_or_registration_offset_visible` is `yes`. For priority 2, either brightness condition may satisfy the controlled-score requirement. `uncertain` never counts as `yes`, and the shared `asset-000112` is counted once per method but only once within a method-family count.

The diagnostic decision is applied without a weighted score:

- `PROCEED TO ONE BOUNDED FOLLOW-UP PREREGISTRATION` when at least one family is supported; choose the first supported family by the fixed priority above;
- `STOP: NO BOUNDED DIAGNOSTIC SIGNAL` when all required evidence is complete and no family is supported;
- `INCONCLUSIVE: REQUIRED EVIDENCE INCOMPLETE` when the authorized inputs are intact but a required review or score collection cannot be completed; or
- `INVALID: BOUNDARY VIOLATION` when an unselected final-test image is accessed, a protected v0.2 artifact is changed, the completed final test is rescored, or forbidden metadata is shown during first-pass observation.

Support means that one observable family deserves a separately designed experiment. It does not establish causality, validate a method change, or authorize deployment. Localized or diffuse-change tags are descriptive only in v0.3.0 and cannot independently authorize a method modification.

## Leakage and Change Controls

- The completed v0.2 final test may not be scored again, used to select a threshold, used to fit a model, or used to compare a changed method.
- Only the 28 fixed selected assets may be visually accessed; their scores remain immutable parent evidence.
- The 60-image diagnostic partition contains only prior development normals and cannot be reported as a held-out performance set.
- Visual observations may not alter the perturbations, method parameters, metrics, signal thresholds, or family priority.
- Probe results may not change the review inventory or observation fields.
- No new anomaly label, synthetic anomaly, segmentation mask, or pixel-level annotation may enter the probe.
- A defect in implementation before any diagnostic image access requires correction and a new controlling commit.
- After any selected image is opened or any diagnostic score is materialized, a substantive rule change requires a new preregistration ID and preservation of the stopped attempt.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before image access or scoring if:

1. the controlling preregistration commit or document identity is missing;
2. any parent artifact, failure-selection file, manifest, external image, fitted state, or dependency identity differs;
3. the exact 28-asset review set or 60-image normal partition cannot be reconstructed;
4. the blind review interface exposes protected method, class, score, rank, or semantic-path metadata;
5. review or probe output already exists; or
6. execution would overwrite any v0.2 or v0.3 evidence.

During execution, preserve and stop on an unauthorized asset access, missing record, duplicate key, order mismatch, non-finite score, transform mismatch, score-range violation, output overwrite, or state/hash mismatch. A stopped or negative study remains publishable evidence.
