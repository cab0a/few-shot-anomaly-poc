# v0.2.7 Label Reveal, Metrics, and Failure Cases

## 日本語概要

## English Summary

The v0.2.7 stage crossed the sealed label boundary once after the complete score-side evidence was committed and pushed. It joined all 200 records by exact ordered opaque asset ID, calculated the fixed image-level metrics for three methods, and mechanically selected the highest-score false positives and lowest-score false negatives. No score, threshold, status, failure code, or prediction changed; no image pixel was read or displayed; and no hard-gate decision was generated.

## Image-Level Results

Anomaly is the positive class. AUROC and AUPRC are descriptive; normal FPR and anomaly recall are the preregistered operating-point measurements.

| Method | AUROC | AUPRC | Normal FPR | Anomaly recall | TP | FN | TN | FP | Score failures |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `ecc_residual` | `0.6640` | `0.6258` | `0.04` | `0.13` | 13 | 87 | 96 | 4 | 0 |
| `patch_hog_ocsvm` | `0.7147` | `0.6983` | `0.05` | `0.16` | 16 | 84 | 95 | 5 | 0 |
| `dinov2_vits14_224_nn` | `0.7943` | `0.7572` | `0.07` | `0.34` | 34 | 66 | 93 | 7 | 0 |

DINOv2 produced the highest descriptive ranking metrics and anomaly recall among the fixed candidates, but its normal FPR was above the preregistered `0.05` maximum and its recall remained far below the `0.90` minimum. ECC residual and Patch HOG met the numeric FPR boundary exactly or better, but their recalls were `0.13` and `0.16`. No method simultaneously met both operating-point conditions.

These comparisons do not constitute the final decision. AUROC and AUPRC cannot waive an operating-point failure, and no weighted aggregate is introduced.

## Mechanical Failure Selection

Selection used no image pixels:

- false positives: anomaly score descending, then opaque asset ID ascending;
- false negatives: anomaly score ascending, then opaque asset ID ascending; and
- maximum five records per error type and method.

| Method | Selected false positives | Selected false negatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ecc_residual` | `asset-000141`, `asset-000090`, `asset-000169`, `asset-000046` | `asset-000095`, `asset-000117`, `asset-000027`, `asset-000112`, `asset-000087` |
| `patch_hog_ocsvm` | `asset-000062`, `asset-000171`, `asset-000172`, `asset-000106`, `asset-000143` | `asset-000044`, `asset-000155`, `asset-000102`, `asset-000111`, `asset-000003` |
| `dinov2_vits14_224_nn` | `asset-000078`, `asset-000093`, `asset-000055`, `asset-000047`, `asset-000104` | `asset-000112`, `asset-000151`, `asset-000156`, `asset-000174`, `asset-000168` |

ECC residual had four total false positives, so all four were selected. Each failure record preserves the opaque ID, revealed source path, true class, unchanged prediction, score, threshold, margin, score status, failure code, and deterministic rank.

No selected or unselected image was opened. The repository therefore makes no claim that alignment, illumination, component layout, defect size, texture, or another visible factor caused any error.

## Known Limitations

- Results cover one category, one official split, one fixed reference set, and one final-test order.
- No confidence interval, significance test, repeated reference selection, or cross-category estimate is provided.
- The three methods use different score scales; raw score magnitude is not comparable across methods.
- No image review or causal failure taxonomy is included in this stage.
- Selected cases are deterministic examples of fixed-threshold errors, not proof of a general failure mechanism.
- Image-level metrics do not measure localization, and no mask or pixel-level metric is included.
- The results do not generalize to production cameras, new products, or other defect distributions.
