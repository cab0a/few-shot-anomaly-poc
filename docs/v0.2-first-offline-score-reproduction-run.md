## Decision Interpretation

The preregistered reproducibility condition requires matching identities, offline execution, finite ordered scores, and maximum absolute difference no greater than `1e-6`. All clauses passed, so condition 9 passes.

This does not by itself return the final preflight `PROCEED`. Condition 10 still requires a finding that the opaque, untouched `pcb2` evaluation boundary remains feasible. A final ordered preflight decision must apply conditions 1 through 10 without allowing this favorable reproduction result to waive another condition.

Even a final preflight `PROCEED` would authorize only a separate v0.2 method-and-evaluation preregistration. It would not establish DINOv2 anomaly accuracy or adoption.

## Limitations

- The comparison uses deterministic synthetic noise, not inspection images.
- Exact equality was observed in one fresh process on the same recorded software and CPU environment.
- The result does not establish portability across PyTorch builds, CPUs, operating systems, or dependency versions.
- The result verifies scalar reproducibility for 10 fixed inputs, not anomaly ranking, threshold transfer, defect recall, or production behavior.
