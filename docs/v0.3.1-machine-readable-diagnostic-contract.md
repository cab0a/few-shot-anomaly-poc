## Encoded Review Boundary

The contract fixes exactly 28 unique opaque asset IDs and 29 method-case records. `asset-000112` is the only shared asset and must be reviewed once. The first-pass order is opaque asset ID ascending.

The first-pass interface is governed by a positive allowlist. It may expose only:

- the opaque `asset_id`;
- the image pixels at the original decoded extent; and
- a blank fixed observation form.

The allowlist is controlling even if a protected concept appears under an alias not enumerated in the negative list. Method, case type, class, prediction, score, threshold, margin, rank, source or semantic path, and cross-method selection information are not first-pass inputs. The separate 29-row linkage contract is explicitly post-review state and cannot be used by the reviewer interface.

This is procedural single-reviewer blinding. The public preregistration already contains the selected opaque IDs, and image content may reveal an apparent defect. The contract does not claim information-theoretic secrecy or inter-rater validation.

## Observation Contract

Each of the 28 assets has one row with the fixed preregistered fields:

- `asset_id` and `review_status`;
- seven `yes` / `no` / `uncertain` visibility fields;
- `visible_observation`; and
- nullable `inferred_cause`.

Unknown fields, protected-field injection, duplicate IDs, missing rows, extra rows, and order differences are rejected. The schema fixes a later review-completion checkpoint so the observation file must be complete and hash-bound before method-case metadata may be joined.

## Encoded Normal Probe

The configuration preserves the exact selection prefix, seed `42`, lowercase SHA-256 ranking, POSIX path tie-breaker, and count of 60 from the existing 881-image normal calibration pool. It fixes:

- `control`;
- `brightness_085`;
- `brightness_115`;
- `gaussian_blur`; and
- `translate_4_4`.

The three unchanged v0.2 fitted-state hashes and normal-only thresholds are bound in the configuration. The planned shape remains 60 base records × 5 conditions × 3 methods = 900 score records. No transform or scorer is executed in v0.3.1.

## Machine-Enforced Stops

The validator rejects, among other changes:

- a changed preregistration or parent-evidence identity;
- an expanded or reordered review inventory;
- a changed fitted-state hash or threshold;
- a changed transform, signal threshold, or family priority;
- a relaxed image-access, rescoring, overwrite, or timestamp rule;
- a protected field in the blind observation schema;
- invalid opaque IDs, hashes, relative paths, enums, or note lengths;
- duplicate or out-of-order blind records; and
- any pre-access checkpoint claiming image access, decoding, display, diagnostic scoring, v0.2 rescoring, or raw data in Git.

The schema defines only the required public evidence files. Raw data, derived images, model state, and semantic-path staging remain outside Git.

## Synthetic Verification Scope

The contract tests use constructed dictionaries only. They establish that the configuration and schema cross-validate and that representative mutations are rejected. They do not decode an image, execute an anomaly method, assess human blinding, or produce a VisA diagnostic result.
