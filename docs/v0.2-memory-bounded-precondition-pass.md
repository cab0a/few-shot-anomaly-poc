## What the RAM Values Mean

The checker recorded two equivalent total-memory observations:

- `os.sysconf("SC_PHYS_PAGES") * os.sysconf("SC_PAGE_SIZE")`: `4,045,004,800` bytes; and
- `/proc/meminfo` `MemTotal`: `4,045,004,800` bytes.

`SC_PHYS_PAGES` reports the physical pages exposed by the running kernel, and `SC_PAGE_SIZE` reports bytes per page. Their product is total RAM visible to WSL. It is not current process usage, free RAM, or a prediction of whether the workload will complete.

Attempt 2 also recorded `3,086,802,944` bytes of `MemAvailable`, `1,073,741,824` bytes of `SwapTotal`, and the same amount of `SwapFree`. These values are machine-state diagnostics and are not acceptance results.

The historical `4,045,017,088`-byte snapshot remains recorded. The current total is still 12,288 bytes below it, so the new artifact does not hide or round the difference. It records `total_ram_meets_superseded_snapshot: false` and `total_ram_is_gating: false` separately.
